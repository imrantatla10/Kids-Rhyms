You live on one side of a river. Your favorite pizza place is on the other side. And you can't swim.

So you throw in a row of big stones and hop across. Until one of them rolls... and you're soaking wet.

So you chop down a giant tree and let it crash across the water. Now you can tiptoe over the trunk. But it's round, it spins, and you wobble like a tightrope walker.

So you roll a second tree right next to it, and nail flat planks across both. Finally, a real walkway!

Then you try to drive your truck over it... and the middle sags toward the water.

So you hammer thick posts into the riverbed, and lay a beam on top to hold it up from below. Shorter gaps, less bending. [excited] Genius!

Until the current pushes on the posts, and the whole thing starts to sway.

So you nail diagonal braces between them, because triangles don't wobble.

Then your neighbor's goat keeps falling off the side.

So you add railings on both edges.

[excited] Congratulations! You just invented the bridge!
